Restaurant Sales & Customer Analysis
Project Overview

This project analyzes restaurant sales and customer ordering behavior using Microsoft Excel and Power BI.

The objective was to transform restaurant order data into meaningful business insights by analyzing revenue, order volume, menu-item performance, food categories, customer ordering patterns, and spending behavior.

Key Insights
Italian generated the highest category revenue at approximately $49K.
Asian recorded the highest order volume at approximately 2.6K orders.
Hamburger was the most ordered menu item with 622 orders.
Cheese Quesadillas recorded the lowest volume among the five least-ordered items with 233 orders.
